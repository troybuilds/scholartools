Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Your privacy is important to ScholarTools. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit our website and use our free educational calculators and resources.

Information We Collect

You can use ScholarTools calculators without creating an account. Information you enter into a calculator is used to provide the requested calculation. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

We may receive technical and usage information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location derived from an IP address, and interactions with the website. The information collected depends on the services and technologies in use.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use ScholarTools and to improve our website. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage and device information. The information collected depends on the analytics services enabled on the website.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for website functionality, analytics, advertising, and measuring the performance of advertisements. These technologies may store or access information in your browser or device.

Advertising and Google AdSense

ScholarTools may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. When advertising services are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and support reporting.

Depending on applicable settings, consent choices, and available advertising options, these technologies may be used to show personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Personalized advertising may use information about a visitor's previous activity to help select advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies in your browser or use web beacons or IP addresses to collect information as a result of advertisements served on this website.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps .

You can manage personalized advertising settings through Google's My Ad Center . You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or advertising functionality.

Third-Party Services

ScholarTools may use third-party services for analytics, advertising, hosting, and website functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services they use.

Data Retention and Security

Information may be retained by third-party service providers according to their own retention policies. We take reasonable steps to maintain the website, but no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

ScholarTools provides educational resources for students. The website is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information. You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings and advertising preferences through available advertising controls. Where required, we will provide appropriate consent choices for cookies and advertising technologies.
