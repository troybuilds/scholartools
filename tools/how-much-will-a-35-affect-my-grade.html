How Much Will a 35 Affect My Grade?

A score of 35% can noticeably reduce your course grade depending on how much the assignment is worth. Use our calculator below to estimate your new average.

🎯 Example

  • Your current grade: 88%
  • The assignment is worth: 20%
  • You score: 35%

After this score, your grade would drop to about 77%.

📉 Why It Drops

Even though 35% is higher than a 25%, it is still well below average and will lower your overall grade if the assignment has significant weight.
